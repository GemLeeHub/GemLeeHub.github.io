寂静的朋友 (II)
Silent Friend (2)  ·  Gem Lee  ·  2026
The first album followed the tree. This one follows the people.
Eight characters from Ildikó Enyedi's film Silent Friend (2025) — a ginkgo in a German university garden, three generations: 1908, 1972, 2020. Each track is one person's inner world: what they wanted, what they held back, how they moved through time in the tree's presence. Nylon-string guitar and voice, in the South American street tradition of Violeta Parra and Chavela Vargas.
