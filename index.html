Working Music II · Soul on the Loose

The body stays at the desk. The soul goes wherever it wants. Fifteen jazz instrumentals for working hours and wandering minds — piano and archtop guitar, mostly without drums, mostly without resolution. Three movements: a settling in, a long drift, a quiet return. The music does not ask for your attention. It assumes you have left already, and it keeps playing anyway.
